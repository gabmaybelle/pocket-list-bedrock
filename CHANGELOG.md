# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first Bedrock release of Pocket List. Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.60 or newer, no experiments.

- A checklist in a corner of your screen, on a dark backdrop: each line reads "24/64 Oak Planks" and
  turns green as you gather it, with a chime and a note when the whole list is done.
- The list screen: open it with the Pocket List Notebook (two paper and string) or `/pocket_list:open`.
  Keep several lists, rename them, change amounts, search any item by name, and clear off what you've
  gathered. Sneak and use the notebook (or `/pocket_list:toggle`) to hide and show the corner list.
- Paste List: a new list from text you type or paste, one item per line or between semicolons, such
  as "64 oak planks; 16 torches". Copy List shows the list as text to copy.
- Counts your inventory and offhand, and what's in the chests, barrels, shulker boxes, hoppers,
  dispensers and droppers you've opened within 32 blocks, as they are right now.
- "Any planks" lines: `#minecraft:planks` in Paste List or the search box counts every kind.
- Settings: the corner list on or off, which corner, how many lines, and whether nearby containers count.
- Your lists are your own: saved on your player in each world, not shared with other players.
